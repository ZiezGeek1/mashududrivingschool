About us

A reputable driving academy, built on 300+ graduates.

Mashudu Driving School was founded in 2017 to provide a genuine driving course experience. We're based in Mpumalanga, operating across the Evander area, and registered with The States of African Nations Association of Driving Schools (S.A.N.A.D.S).

We offer training programmes for drivers of all ages and experience levels, so every moment you spend with us contributes to a rewarding future of driving. Our professional instructors help you feel comfortable and confident as you develop your skills on the road.

Our academy is led day-to-day by a director who is fully involved, committed and devoted to daily operations — keeping instructors current on the latest K53 techniques and holding every lesson to a consistent standard.

Proudly women-owned and led

Our mission

Teaching today's drivers with tomorrow's tools.

We create an interactive driver programme loaded with e-learning, preparing learners practically and psychologically with the skills they need for the road — enhancing safe driving, reducing accident rates, and helping nervous drivers pass the first time.

Our vision

South Africa's leading driving academy.

To become the leading driving academy in South Africa with multiple branches nationally, state-of-the-art driving simulator technology, hands-on e-learning stations and top-class instructors — awarding 10 underprivileged students driving courses every year, with our own private testing centre.

What we offer

Theory and practical training, done properly.

We place equal emphasis on the rules of the road and on a student driver's behaviour and attitude — building safety and courtesy into every lesson, in line with K53.

Theory classes

Traffic rules, principles of safe driving, vehicle control, avoiding road hazards, dealing with other road users, and driving ethics — all taught in line with the K53 syllabus.

Practical classes

A certified K53 instructor trains you to drive, control the vehicle and handle the road. Duration is tailored to your level and how quickly you build confidence behind the wheel.

Community & accreditation

Giving back, and doing it by the book.

In 2018, we taught learner's licences through to full licences for 10 orphans referred to us by government — one step toward our goal of awarding 10 underprivileged students driving courses every year.

We also give back throughout the year by running seasonal specials, making lessons more accessible to the community during key times of year.

We're registered with The States of African Nations Association of Driving Schools (S.A.N.A.D.S), and our human resource policy keeps us staffed with a careful blend of experienced instructors and youth development, all operating within the rules and regulations of the trade.
